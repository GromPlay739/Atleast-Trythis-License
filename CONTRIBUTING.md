# Contributing to ATL

Thank you for your interest in improving Atleast Trythis License (ATL).

ATL is a license text, so proposed changes can affect the rights and obligations of creators and users. Please keep proposals clear, specific, and mindful of both the Russian and English editions.

## How to propose a change

1. Open an issue describing the problem or proposed improvement.
2. Identify the relevant section and explain why it should change.
3. If possible, provide suggested wording in both Russian and English.
4. Explain whether the proposal changes the scope of permissions, attribution requirements, treatment of derivative works, versioning, or other legal effects.

## Translation and consistency

- The Russian and English editions are intended to be equally authoritative.
- Changes to one edition should be reviewed against the other edition.
- Do not silently introduce a substantive difference between language editions.
- If a wording change may alter legal meaning, explain the intended effect.

## Versioning

- Changes that affect rights or obligations should be considered for a new license version rather than silently changing an existing published version.
- Published versions should remain available in the repository.
- A later version must not be described as retroactively removing rights already granted under an earlier version.

## Scope

This repository maintains the ATL license text and related explanatory materials. A contribution does not itself grant the contributor ownership of ATL or authority to make legal representations on behalf of other contributors.

## Legal review

Contributions are not legal advice. The maintainers may request independent legal review before accepting changes that affect legal interpretation.
